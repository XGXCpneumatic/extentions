Free Chrome extensions

Little extensions for a sillier web

A panda that eats bamboo while it watches you, a bingo card for the whole internet, and a few other tiny things to make browsing more fun.

1Download & unzip

Click a download button below, then right-click the .zip and choose Extract All.

2Turn on Developer mode

Go to chrome://extensions and flip Developer mode on (top right).

3Load unpacked

Click Load unpacked and pick the folder you just unzipped. Done!

Move your mouse around. He's watching.

🐼 Bamboo Panda

A little panda sits in the corner of every page, munching bamboo and following your cursor with his eyes.

  • Takes bites, drops crumbs, grabs a fresh stalk when he's done
  • Fades out when your cursor gets close, so he never blocks anything
  • Pick left or right corner and small, medium or large
